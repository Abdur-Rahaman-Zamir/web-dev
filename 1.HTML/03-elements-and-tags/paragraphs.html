INSHA-ALLAH

Allah Subhanahu wa Taala is the One and Only, the Creator of the heavens and the earth, The Sustainer of all that exists. He is Al-Rahman (The Most Merciful) and Al-Rahim (The Most Compassionate), whose mercy encompasses everything. All praise (Hamd) belongs solely to Him, for He is perfect in His attributes, free from all defects, and beyond any comparison. His power, wisdom, and knowledge have no limits, and His decree is always just. We glorify Him (Sana) by declaring His greatness, saying SubhanAllah (Glory be to Allah), Alhamdulillah (All praise is for Allah), Allahu Akbar (Allah is the Greatest), for He alone deserves worship and To remember Allah in Hamd and Sana is to fill the heart with peace and the tongue with truth.
